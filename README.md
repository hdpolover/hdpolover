I build mobile apps in Flutter and the backends they talk to. Most projects I take on span the app, the API, the database and the deployment, so I tend to own the whole path rather than one slice of it.

Booking platforms, payment flows, community apps, exam systems. Plus the occasional hard problem picked up on purpose, to learn the internals rather than the wrapper.

Been pushing commits since 2018, currently around 13,000 of them. Most of the interesting ones are in private repos.

**The work itself lives at [hendracodes.com](https://hendracodes.com).** Case studies, stack notes and a contact form are all there.
